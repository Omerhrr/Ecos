     1→# ECOS
     2→
     3→## Luxeen's Global E-Commerce Operating System
     4→
     5→**Built by Plannexis**
     6→
     7→> **Ecos gives e-commerce businesses everything they need to source, market, sell, fulfill, deliver, and manage their business from one place.**
     8→
     9→---
    10→
    11→# 1. Executive Summary
    12→
    13→Ecos is a global e-commerce operating system built exclusively for Luxeen.
    14→
    15→It is not a generic SaaS marketplace, a supplier directory, a Shopify clone, a standalone logistics platform, or simply a dropshipping application.
    16→
    17→Ecos is the infrastructure through which Luxeen operates a global commerce network connecting suppliers, e-commerce operators, logistics infrastructure, payment systems, customers, and AI-powered business operations.
    18→
    19→The first commercial corridor is:
    20→
    21→**China → Nigeria**
    22→
    23→However, China → Nigeria is the starting network, not the permanent definition of Ecos.
    24→
    25→The architecture is designed from the beginning to support multiple countries, currencies, supply markets, logistics corridors, payment systems, and eventually bidirectional commerce.
    26→
    27→The central idea is simple:
    28→
    29→> **The e-commerce operator runs the business. Ecos runs the infrastructure underneath it.**
    30→
    31→An operator should be able to discover a product, evaluate it, add it to their store, create a sales page, market it, acquire customers, manage leads, create orders, coordinate fulfillment, track delivery, handle payments and returns, and understand their business performance without assembling a collection of disconnected third-party tools.
